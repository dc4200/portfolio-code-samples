Frequently Asked Questions

See All FAQs
What is a custom home?

If you're looking for a brand new home, but haven't been able to find the right one to fit your needs, you might want to consider building a custom home. Custom homes are brand new homes that are built and designed to a homeowner's unique specifications.

What advantages do new homes have over used houses?

First of all - no compromises! Every used house on the market is going to have something that's just “not right” for you, something you have to settle for. With a fully customized home, every square foot is built exactly the way you want it. And includes everything you want, such as messy kitchens, open floor plans, a huge pantry, or spa bathrooms - no compromises like with existing homes. Also, all of our homes are built with the latest energy efficient products that help to save money on your utility bills every month.

Do you have models to tour?

Yes, our model homes and design studio locations are open to the public every day we're open. You are welcome to stop in and browse our homes without an appointment in a no-pressure environment.

Can you help us find land if we don't already own land?

Don't worry if you don't have land yet. Almost half of our customers did not own land before they came to us. We have valuable relationships with the real estate community in each of our building areas, who will be happy to help you find the perfect lot for your home.

Will Schumacher Homes handle my site work?

We sure will! Unlike some builders who expect you to prepare your site and have it "build ready" before they start, we do all of that for you. Since every lot is different as part of your lot walk and quote, we will estimate the costs involved to get your site ready for your new home, but rest assured - all the work will be handled by us. This is the best way to ensure that your home gets perfectly situated on your land. We can also address any concerns or questions you might have about your lot before the actual building of your home takes place to make it as easy and carefree as possible.
